Privacy Policy

This is a private, single-user application. It collects no personal data and operates no servers of its own.

When authorised, it accesses Google Nest device data (camera events, live views, thermostat state) solely to display and automate that data inside the owner's self-hosted Home Assistant instance. Data is processed on the owner's own hardware, never stored, shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. Revoking the OAuth grant at any time ends all access.
